## Brand & Style

This design system channels an artisanal, sun-drenched culinary warmth inspired by raw terracotta, hand-ground spices, tanned leather, and rich roasted espresso. It addresses connoisseurs of gourmet dining, boutique hospitality, natural living, and crafted interior design. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Tactile Minimalism**:
- Generous cream whitespace evokes heavy textured paper and linen surfaces.
- Earth-fired chromatic accents (terracotta, ochre, roasted coffee) infuse grounded sophistication rather than synthetic glow.
- Surfaces feel physical, calm, and deliberate—striking a balance between Mediterranean warmth, rustic culinary comfort, and contemporary editorial refinement.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture draws directly from natural clays and aromatic spices, anchored by warm, high-legibility neutrals:

- **Primary (`#D6834F`)**: Sun-baked terracotta clay. Used for primary actions, prominent hero focal points, and key interactive badges.
- **Secondary (`#A35233`)**: Burnt sienna and spiced cinnamon. Serves as active interactive states, high-contrast links, and secondary accents.
- **Tertiary (`#E6AE74`)**: Golden caramel and sand ochre. Highlights gentle accents, selected container states, and soft culinary tags.
- **Neutral Base & Ink (`#42281C`)**: Dark roast espresso / chocolate brown. Replaces sterile pitch black for all primary typography, distinct icons, and crisp linear contours.
- **Canvas & Containers**:
  - Main Canvas: `#FDF8F3` (warm unbleached silk and cream).
  - Surface Subdued: `#F6D2AC` at 20–35% opacity or solid `#F5EBE1` for soft cards, table stripes, and inset wells.
  - Surface Crisp: `#FFFFFF` strictly reserved for elevated overlay cards needing clean contrast against the cream canvas.

## Typography

Epilogue anchors the entire typographic scale. Its slightly geometric proportion combined with editorial warmth captures handcrafted, deliberate craftsmanship without sacrificing modern digital clarity.

- **Display & Headlines**: Generous weights (`600`, `700`) paired with tight negative letter-spacing give headlines an authoritative, culinary editorial character reminiscent of artisanal food journals.
- **Body & Captions**: Standard weights (`400`) set in warm espresso (`#42281C`) ensure effortless readability against cream backdrops.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Slightly tracking-expanded (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) medium and semi-bold weights for badges, metadata, and button labels ensure tactile crispness even at small physical sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes expansive, composed breathing room:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: A structured 12-column grid with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Elements feel unhurried, echoing an art exhibition catalog or luxury recipe journal.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: An 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A 4-column layout utilizing `margin-sm: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter-sm: 1rem` (16px) to maximize usable viewport space while safeguarding soft edges.
- **Rhythm**: All margins, gap structures, and internal cell paddings strictly anchor to multiples of 4px and 8px, maintaining harmonic alignment across all responsive breakpoints.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **warm tonal layering** and **cinnamon-tinted ambient shadows**, avoiding sterile grey drop-shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Uncoated surface `#FDF8F3`.
- **Level 1 (Soft Containers & Wells)**: Warm sand `#F5EBE1` or translucent `#F6D2AC` at 25% with zero shadow, separated by hairline warm borders (`#E6AE74` at 40% opacity).
- **Level 2 (Cards & Tiles)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` or elevated `#FFFDF9` surface with an ambient spice drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(66, 40, 28, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(163, 82, 51, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Popovers & Floating Actions)**: Crisp warm white with deeper ambient depth:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(66, 40, 28, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(163, 82, 51, 0.08);`
- **Focus Rings & Highlighting**: Subtle dual rings consisting of a 2px offset in `#FDF8F3` and an outer 2px solid ring in `#D6834F`.

## Shapes

The geometry strikes a balance between crafted stoneware and contemporary product design:

- **Standard Elements (inputs, buttons, items)**: `0.5rem` (8px) radius, conveying softened terracotta tiles and smooth wood edges.
- **Cards, Panels & Groupings (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px) radius to create inviting, approachable enclosures.
- **Hero Containers & Dialogs (`rounded-xl`)**: `1.5rem` (24px) radius for fluid, organic containment.
- **Pills**: Used strictly for metadata chips, category tags, and notification counters to differentiate dynamic markers from structural interaction blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Terracotta (`#D6834F`) background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to Burnt Sienna (`#A35233`). Active state scales slightly (0.98).
- **Secondary / Outlined**: Transparent background with a 1.5px solid border in `#42281C` or `#D6834F`, text in `#42281C`. Hover fills with `#F6D2AC` at 25% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Text in `#A35233` with an understated hover fill of `#F5EBE1`.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped badges using `#F5EBE1` background with `#42281C` text for default states.
- Active or filtered chips employ `#D6834F` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- Micro tags for status or seasonal highlights use warm ochre `#E6AE74` with dark chocolate `#42281C` text.

### Cards
- Constructed with `rounded-lg` (16px) corners on elevated `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9`.
- Framed with an ultra-soft 1px border (`rgba(230, 174, 116, 0.35)`).
- Media headers (culinary imagery, textiles, pottery) carry seamless top-corner matching border-radii.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form inputs feature `#FFFDF9` backgrounds, `#42281C` text, and a 1px border in `#E6AE74`.
- On focus: the border transitions smoothly to `#D6834F` with a soft 3px ambient ring of `rgba(214, 131, 79, 0.2)`.
- Checkboxes and Radio buttons use `#D6834F` when checked, with an inner `#FFFFFF` glyph or dot, set against smooth 8px and circular bounds respectively.

### Lists & Dividers
- Separator rules employ 1px solid lines tinted in `rgba(66, 40, 28, 0.08)`.
- List item hovers gently illuminate with `#F6D2AC` at 20% opacity and smooth 8px corner radii.
